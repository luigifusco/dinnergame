# Rising Stakes ingredients

The autumn pantry for eight players: **80 ingredient cards** for 32 ingredients,
which [print](README.md#print-the-cards) on ten A4 sheets, one tier per sheet.
Each card is a voucher for one portion, and two copies of a card make a double
portion. Swap ingredients for the season and the guests, but keep the tier and
label counts. Olive oil, flaky salt, black pepper, and brine are free and aren't
cards.

The [concept draft](FOCACCIA_PLAN.md#ingredient-cards) explains tiers, the OVEN
and FINISH labels, and pan capacity.

## The deck

| Tier | Copies | Ingredients | Cards | OVEN | FINISH |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Common | 4 of each | 12 | 48 | 36 | 12 |
| Fine | 2 of each | 12 | 24 | 16 | 8 |
| Luxury | 1 of each | 8 | 8 | 2 | 6 |
| **Total** | | **32** | **80** | **54** | **26** |

Eight focaccias hold 48 OVEN and 24 FINISH cards, so there is always something
left to trade.

Portions are sized for a 20 cm round pan and a 250 g dough ball. One card is
noticeable on its own, and six OVEN cards make a generously topped focaccia.
Cards show only the art and the name, so each label and portion goes on the
pantry bowl and on a printed copy of these tables. The cards print from
`cards\ingredients.yaml`; keep its names, tiers, and copies in step with these
tables.

### Combinations

The deck supports classic combinations without making them rules: tomatoes,
olives, and oregano; potato, rosemary, and onion; pear, gorgonzola, walnuts,
and honey; grapes and rosemary; pumpkin and sausage; 'nduja and honey. The
dream is mortadella, stracciatella, and pistachio: three Luxury cards that
each exist only once.

## Diets and allergens

Check these against your guests' diets and allergies before shopping. The
[host notes](FOCACCIA_PLAN.md#host-notes) explain what can stay in the deck.

| Contains | Cards |
|---|---|
| Nuts | Pesto, walnuts, and pistachio. Mortadella may contain pistachios too. |
| Fish | Anchovies. |
| Pork | Sausage, guanciale, 'nduja, mortadella, and prosciutto crudo. |
| Milk | Mozzarella, gorgonzola, parmigiano, burrata, stracciatella, and most pesto. |

Parmigiano and gorgonzola are traditionally made with animal rennet, and so is
the cheese in most pesto, so strict vegetarians may trade those away too.
