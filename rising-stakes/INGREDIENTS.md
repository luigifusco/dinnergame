# Rising Stakes ingredients

The autumn pantry for eight players: **44 ingredient cards**, one per
ingredient, which [print](README.md#print-the-cards) on five A4 sheets. Every
card is equal and exists only once. The deck runs from classics like cherry
tomatoes and burrata to the experimental, the scandalous, and the straight-up
bad, like ketchup and gummy bears.

Each card is a voucher for one portion. Swap ingredients for the season and the
guests, but keep about the same OVEN and FINISH counts. Olive oil, flaky salt,
black pepper, and brine are free and aren't cards.

The [concept draft](FOCACCIA_PLAN.md#ingredient-cards) explains the OVEN and
FINISH labels and pan capacity.

## The deck

| Label | Cards |
|---|---|
| OVEN | 25 |
| FINISH | 19 |
| **Total** | **44** |

Eight players get about five cards each, three OVEN and two FINISH, well short
of a pan's capacity, so every card counts.

Portions are sized for a 20 cm round pan and a 250 g dough ball. One card is
noticeable on its own, and six OVEN cards make a generously topped focaccia.
Cards show only the art and the name, so each label and portion goes on the
pantry bowl and on a printed copy of these tables. The cards print from
`cards\ingredients.yaml`; keep its names in step with these tables.

### Combinations

The deck supports classic combinations without making them rules: tomatoes,
olives, and oregano; potato, rosemary, and onion; pear, gorgonzola, walnuts,
and honey; grapes and rosemary; pumpkin and sausage; 'nduja and honey. The
dream is mortadella, stracciatella, and pistachio, and since every card exists
only once, only one focaccia can have it.

## Diets and allergens

Check these against your guests' diets and allergies before shopping. The
[host notes](FOCACCIA_PLAN.md#host-notes) explain what can stay in the deck.

| Contains | Cards |
|---|---|
| Nuts | Pesto, walnuts, pistachio, and Nutella. Mortadella may contain pistachios too. |
| Fish | Anchovies. Most kimchi contains fish sauce or shrimp. |
| Pork | Sausage, guanciale, 'nduja, mortadella, and prosciutto crudo. Most gummy bears contain pork gelatin. |
| Milk | Mozzarella, gorgonzola, parmigiano, burrata, stracciatella, Nutella, and most pesto. Dark chocolate may contain milk. |
| Soy | Miso. |
| Celery | Celery. |

Parmigiano and gorgonzola are traditionally made with animal rennet, and so is
the cheese in most pesto, so strict vegetarians may trade those away too.
