# Wild ingredients: draft

Ideas for wild, experimental, and straight-up bad ingredient cards. Delete what
you don't like, add your own, and leave notes anywhere. Nothing here is part of
the game yet, and labels and portions are first guesses.

Format: **Name** (label): portion. Comment.

## Experimental

They sound wrong, but one of these could win the vote.

- **Nutella** (FINISH): a heaped teaspoon. Go full dessert.
- **Lemon** (OVEN): four paper-thin slices. Brilliant with rosemary.
- **Persimmon** (OVEN): half a cachi, sliced. Autumn's strangest fruit.
- **Pomegranate** (FINISH): a spoonful of seeds.
- **Dark chocolate** (FINISH): one square, grated, with extra flaky salt.
- **Chili crisp** (FINISH): a teaspoon of crunchy chili oil.

## Jokers

Cards that bend the pantry rules instead of being a topping.

- **Avanzi**: at assembly, take any one portion left in the pantry.
- **Sorpresa**: a covered bowl that only the host has seen. Bake it, or send it
  back unopened.
- **Doppio**: double the portion of another card you play.
- **Ci pensa la Nonna**: the host tops one quarter of your focaccia, any way
  they like.

## How they could work

Options, not decisions. Keep, delete, or combine.

- **Free slots.** Each of the ten sheets has a blank spare, so ten wild cards
  print for free: six with a red back, three green, and one blue. Each hides
  in its tier's pile.
- **Ambush.** Shuffle the crimes and bad cards into the Common pile, so any
  random draw can be one.
- **Own back.** Give wild cards a back color of their own, so everyone can see
  who's holding them. This rules out the free slots.
- **Bake bonus.** Baking a crime or a bad card pays 3 beans and doesn't use a
  topping slot.
- **Hot potato.** Whoever still holds a bad card at Last Call pays 2 beans, so
  bluff it away.
- **Il Disastro.** The most chaotic focaccia wins a Luxury card instead of a
  burnt breadstick.

As with every card, nothing goes on a focaccia without its owner's consent.
Check new allergens before shopping: Nutella (nuts and milk), fish fingers
(fish), Fernet (alcohol), and anything else you add.
