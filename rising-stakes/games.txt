La Patata Bollente (chance): pass a parcel to music; when it stops, unwrap a layer and obey its note (draw, steal, give). A Luxury waits in the core.
Roulette Calabrese (chance): eight olives, one hiding a fiery chili; everyone eats one at once, and whoever gets the fire wins a Luxury.
La Bottiglia (chance, steal): spin an empty wine bottle; whoever it points at hands you a blind card. Everyone spins once.
Tombola (chance, in the draft): Italian bingo; ambo and terno win a Common, up to a Luxury for tombola.
Il Forno (push your luck, in the draft): roll two dice for a bake total, and stop before a 1 burns it.
Mezzogiorno di Fuoco (duel, steal): a grissino in your belt; draw on "Focaccia!" but not on "Formaggio!". The winner takes a blind card from the loser.
Il Cucchiaio di Legno (free-for-all, steal): the card game Spoons; whoever is left without a spoon gives the first grabber a blind card.
Morra (duel, in the draft): the finger-guessing game, any time, for stakes both players agree on.
Le Bocce (teams): two teams roll mandarins at a cherry tomato across the floor; the winners split a prize pot, if they can agree how.
La Staffetta dei Taralli (teams): pass taralli along grissini held in your mouths, no hands; the fastest team splits a prize pot.
Rischiatutto (teams): food trivia, buzzing in by banging a pan; each right answer adds a card to the team's pot.
Caccia al Tesoro (pairs): the host hides cards around the house, each found by solving a riddle; finders keepers.
Dividi o Rubi (betrayal, steal): pairs share a pot and talk for a minute, then each secretly picks split or steal. A lone thief takes it all; two get nothing.
Il Ladro (hidden role, steal): a secret thief robs by winking, and robbed players drop a blind card in the loot jar; unmask the thief to win it.
La Parola Proibita (all evening, steal): don't say "pizza"; whoever slips loses a blind card to the first player who catches them.
Tre Carte (gambling, in the draft): stake beans and follow a Fine through the host's three-card shuffle.
OK, il Prezzo è Giusto (estimation): guess what a pantry item cost; the closest without going over wins its card.
Naso d'Oro (senses, in the draft): sniff six covered cups and guess the herbs and spices.
Il Pugno (skill, in the draft): scoop 100 g of flour by eye; the closest wins.
Mimo (social, in the draft): act out a Common without words; the first to guess keeps it.
Mise en Place (race, in the draft): strip rosemary or peel garlic for the pantry; the fastest wins.
L'Asta (auction, in the draft): bid beans on a few lots, such as a Luxury or a mystery card.
