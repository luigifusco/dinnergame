WAYS TO WIN A CARD
Random: the winner draws blind from the bank.
Chosen card: a face-up card is picked during the game, and the pick changes the game, such as what gets acted out or how hard the shot is.
Own cards: players stake cards from their hand, and the game reshuffles or hands them out.
Ranking: the game ranks everyone, and the ranking is the picking order from a face-up prize pool.
Steal: the winner draws blind from the loser's hand, or the loser picks what to give.

TEAM GAMES
Team Charades (chosen card): the actor picks one of six face-up cards, some easy to act out and some not, and their team has a minute to guess it. The guesser keeps it; on a miss, the other team gets one guess to steal it.
Three Rounds (ranking): Time's Up! with a stack of ingredient cards: describe freely, then one word only, then mime only. The winning team picks first from the prize pool, alternating with the losing team.
Codenames (chosen card): Codenames on a 4 x 4 grid of ingredient cards. The guesser keeps every card of their team's color they find.
Jeopardy (chosen card): a Jeopardy board of face-up ingredient cards. Pick one and answer a question about it; whoever answers right keeps it, and a wrong answer lets the other team steal it.
Blind Pans (random): upside-down pans on the floor, each hiding a blind card. A blindfolded player, steered only by their team's shouting, taps one with a wooden spoon and keeps what's underneath.
Steal the Bacon (steal): two teams in lines with secret numbers. The host calls a number, and those two race to snatch a tea towel and get home untagged; the winner takes a blind card from the loser.

EVERYONE GAMES
The Critic (own cards): a rotating critic reads a prompt like "what your ex would put on a focaccia". Everyone else plays a card face down, and the critic's favorite takes them all.
Winner Takes the Pot (own cards, gambling): whoever wants in puts one or more cards face up in the pot, one ticket per card. Draw one ticket; it takes the whole pot.
I Doubt It (own cards, bluff): in turn, play a card face down and claim an ingredient later in the alphabet than the last claim. Anyone may shout "I doubt it!" and flip it, and whoever was right takes the whole pile.
Twenty Questions (chosen card): one player secretly picks a card from the bank, and the others share 20 yes-or-no questions. Whoever names it wins it; otherwise the picker keeps it.
Cork Toss (chosen card): glasses in a line, one card in each near glass and three in the farthest. One cork toss each; win the cards in the glass you land in.
The Handful (chosen card): pick one, two, or three face-up cards, then scoop 100 g of flour by eye. You win them within 20 g for one card, 10 g for two, or 5 g for three.
I Went to Market (ranking): the memory chain "I went to the market and bought...", each player adding an ingredient. Slip and you're out; the last one standing picks first.
Blind Tasting (ranking): taste six spoonfuls blindfolded and name them; the best score picks first.
The Merchant's Fair (own cards, final): during the bake, the corner symbols on your cards are the tickets. Needs the symbols.

DUELS FOR EVENTS
Finger Guessing (both show fingers and shout the total)
Odds and Evens
Rock, Paper, Scissors
Thumb War
Staring Contest (blink or laugh and you lose)
Arm Wrestling

EVENTS BETWEEN GAMES
The Swap: two players must swap a card, each choosing what to give.
Blind Swap: two players swap a blind card from each other's hand.
The Duel: two players fight a duel; the winner draws blind from the loser's hand.
The Exchange: one player trades a card of their choice for a blind one from the bank.
Pass Left: everyone passes a card of their choice to the left.
I Cut, You Choose: two players get three cards from the bank. One splits them into two piles, and the other picks a pile.
Robin Hood: whoever holds the most cards gives a blind one to whoever holds the fewest.
The Thief: one player draws a blind card from anyone they like.
The Spy: one player secretly looks at another's whole hand.
The Deal: two players have one minute to agree on a trade; if they don't, each gives a card to the bank.
Windfall: everyone draws a card.
Double: the next game pays two cards instead of one.
Revolution: everyone passes their whole hand to the left.

WHO AND WHEN
Draw an event card after each game.
Spin a bottle to pick the players.
Corner symbols: draw a card from the bank, and the event hits whoever holds its symbol.
