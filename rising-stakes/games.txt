WAYS TO WIN A CARD
Random: the winner draws blind from the bank.
Chosen card: a face-up card is picked during the game, and the pick changes the game, such as what gets acted out or how hard the shot is.
Own cards: players stake cards from their hand, and the game reshuffles or hands them out.
Ranking: the game ranks everyone, and the ranking is the picking order from a face-up prize pool.
Steal: the winner draws blind from the loser's hand, or the loser picks what to give.

TEAM GAMES
Mimo a Squadre (chosen card): charades. The actor picks one of six face-up cards, an easy Common or a tough Wild, and their team has a minute to guess it. The guesser keeps it; on a miss, the other team gets one guess to steal it.
Tre Giri (ranking): Time's Up! with a stack of ingredient cards: describe freely, then one word only, then mime only. The winning team picks first from the prize pool, alternating with the losing team.
Nome in Codice (chosen card): Codenames on a 4 x 4 grid of ingredient cards. The guesser keeps every card of their team's color they find.
Rischiatutto (chosen card): a Jeopardy board of face-up ingredient cards. Pick one and answer a question about it, harder for a Luxury; whoever answers right keeps it, and a wrong answer lets the other team steal it.
Pentolaccia (random): upside-down pans on the floor, each hiding a blind card. A blindfolded player, steered only by their team's shouting, taps one with a wooden spoon and keeps what's underneath.
Rubabandiera (steal): two teams in lines with secret numbers. The host calls a number, and those two race to snatch a tea towel and get home untagged; the winner takes a blind card from the loser.

EVERYONE GAMES
Il Critico (own cards): a rotating critic reads a prompt like "what your ex would put on a focaccia". Everyone else plays a card face down, and the critic's favorite takes them all.
Piatto Ricco (own cards, gambling): "Piatto ricco, mi ci ficco." Whoever wants in puts a card face up in the pot for tickets: 1 for a Common, 2 for a Fine or Wild, 3 for a Luxury. Draw one ticket; it takes the whole pot.
Dubito (own cards, bluff): in turn, play a card face down and claim a tier at least as high as the last one. Anyone may shout "Dubito!" and flip it, and whoever was right takes the whole pile.
Venti Domande (chosen card): one player secretly picks a card from the bank, and the others share 20 yes-or-no questions. Whoever names it wins it; otherwise the picker keeps it.
Il Lancio del Tappo (chosen card): glasses in a line, Commons in the near ones and a Luxury in the farthest. One cork toss each; win the card in the glass you land in.
Il Pugno (chosen card): pick a face-up card, then scoop 100 g of flour by eye. You win it within 20 g for a Common, 10 g for a Fine, or 5 g for a Luxury.
Sono Andato al Mercato (ranking): the memory chain "I went to the market and bought...", each player adding an ingredient. Slip and you're out; the last one standing picks first.
Il Palato (ranking): taste six spoonfuls blindfolded and name them; the best score picks first.
Il Mercante in Fiera (own cards, final): during the bake, the corner symbols on your cards are the tickets. Needs the symbols.

EVENTS BETWEEN GAMES
Lo Scambio: two players must swap a card, each choosing what to give.
Al Buio: two players swap a blind card from each other's hand.
La Sfida: two players fight a duel; the winner draws blind from the loser's hand.
Il Cambio: one player trades a card of their choice for a blind one from the bank.
Giro Tavola: everyone passes a card of their choice to the left.
Dividi e Scegli: two players get three cards from the bank. One splits them into two piles, and the other picks a pile.
Robin Hood: whoever holds the most cards gives a blind one to whoever holds the fewest.
Il Ladro: one player draws a blind card from anyone they like.
La Spia: one player secretly looks at another's whole hand.
Il Patto: two players have one minute to agree on a trade; if they don't, each gives a card to the bank.
Manna: everyone draws a Common.
Doppio: the next game pays two cards instead of one.
Rivoluzione: everyone passes their whole hand to the left.

WHO AND WHEN
Draw an event card after each game.
Spin a bottle to pick the players.
Corner symbols: draw a card from the bank, and the event hits whoever holds its symbol.
